ABOUT US

Metal supply, done right.

A trusted name in ferrous & non-ferrous metals, serving India's core industries with certified quality and dependable stock.

Krishna Metal & Tubes is a leading importer, stockist and supplier of all types of ferrous and non-ferrous metals — and a manufacturer of stainless steel pipes in all grades, shapes and sizes.

With strong technical know-how, we serve project and maintenance requirements across chemicals, fertilizers, petrochemicals, nuclear & thermal power, cement, paper and refineries — delivering quality material on a repeat-order basis, on convenient terms.

Our commitment is simple: certified material, honest rates and on-time dispatch — every single order.
